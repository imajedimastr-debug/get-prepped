10 meals, 1 cooking session

Saving recipes is easy. Turning them into a week of food is the part nobody teaches.

Shopping recipe by recipe means doubled ingredients and half-used groceries. Cooking them one at a time means chopping the same onion three times. Get Prepped plans the whole week as one job.

How it works

Four steps, from a pile of links to containers in the fridge.

Questions

Can I use Get Prepped today?

Not yet. We're building it now. Join the early-access list and we'll email you when it's ready to try.

No. Joining is free and we don't ask for payment details. When the app launches, you decide whether to subscribe.

I've never meal prepped. Is this for me?

Yes, that's who we're building it for. The cooking plan tells you what to do and in what order, starting with the prep that saves the most time.

Which recipes can I add?

We're designing Get Prepped to work with links from recipe sites and food blogs, so the recipes you already like become part of your plan.
